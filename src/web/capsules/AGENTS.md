# Capsules

`src/web/capsules/` (#8, plan section 2): a capsule is a named subset of one wardrobe's garments (office, weekend, a trip's pool). **The closet is implicit**, never a row: every garment in the closet (`inCloset`), what every page shows without a capsule (the list shows it first, linking to the grid). Tables `capsule` (`owner_id`, `name` text not null and trimmed, one per owner in any case through `capsule_owner_id_lower_name_unique` on `(owner_id, lower(name))`, `notes`, `created_at`) and `capsule_garment` (`(capsule_id, garment_id)` primary key, both cascade, index on `garment_id`), `drizzle/0009_capsules.sql`.

- **Part of the shared wardrobe** (owner decision on #8; the plan said private, the owner overrode it): every route takes `?ownerId=` and goes through `authorizeWardrobe`. A VIEW grantee lists and opens the grantor's capsules and filters the grid by one; a MANAGE grantee also changes membership (picker, toggles); only the owner creates, renames and deletes. A capsule outside the addressed wardrobe is a 404 (`capsuleNotFound`), a refused change on a visible one a 403. **Every capsule read of one and every write is `capsule-actions.ts`'s** (#188: `requireCapsule`/`capsuleIn`, `createOwnCapsule`, `renameCapsule`, `deleteOwnCapsule`, `setCapsuleMembers`, `setGarmentCapsules`, each `(db, actor, address, input)`, refusing itself), which the routes and the MCP tools call; a route asks its guard first only to draw a refused form for the right audience. The matrix rows are in `authorization-capsules.spec.ts`, the domain functions' refusals in `capsule-actions.spec.ts`.
- **Routes**: `GET /capsules` (the wardrobe page's Capsules tab: the closet card, then the capsules by name with counts and a strip of their newest four; one statement after the session with the switcher, `capsuleListSql`, each capsule's card a subquery correlated through `inCapsule(capsule.id)`, #170; it was four), `GET /capsules/new`, `POST /capsules` (303 to the capsule, `?created=1`), `GET /capsules/:id` (its members in the closet as the grid's tiles and keyset pages; Choose garments, Filter the wardrobe, Build an outfit), `GET /capsules/:id/edit`, `POST /capsules/:id` (rename and notes; a blank name, or one the owner's other capsule has in any case, re-renders the form 400: `createCapsule`/`updateCapsule` answer the index's unique violation as `'name-taken'`, in a savepoint so the seed's transaction survives), `DELETE /capsules/:id` (`navigateTo` the list; the garments stay), `POST /capsules/:id/garments` (the picker's Save, 303 back with `added`/`removed` for the toast), `POST /wardrobe/:id/capsules` (the garment page's capsules row, an `AutosaveForm` that answers its status line; registered by the capsules plugin).
- **One writer**, `changeMembership` (a Queryable, one transaction, both sides locked `FOR SHARE` in one statement, `lockMembers`: capsules then garments, each in id order; #172, it was one per side): removes the pairings in `remove`, then adds those in `add`; ids outside the wardrobe are dropped. It answers which named capsules and garments are the wardrobe's (`capsules`, `garments`: a wishlist item is found, and never paired), so nothing reads the capsule or garment first: set_capsule_membership (MCP), the picker's Save and the toggles answer the 404 after a write that changed nothing (#172, #251). The picker's Save and a toggle are 6 statements (the session, `begin`, the lock, the delete, the insert, `commit`; they were 7), a refusal 4 (`capsules.spec.ts`). The picker and the toggles post what they **showed** (`shown`) beside what is checked, and only shown-and-unchecked pairings leave: members on pages never scrolled to, filtered out or archived, and capsules made meanwhile, are left alone. The protocol (body schema, `unchecked`, the `ShownId` input) is `src/web/checklist.tsx`, shared with the packing list.
- **Archived garments keep their membership** and are left out of every capsule read (counts, strips, the capsule page, the filter, the picker), like everywhere else.
- **Styling from a capsule**: `/styling?capsule=` (the addressed wardrobe's capsule, so a grantee styles a shared one through `?ownerId=`, browsing only) cycles every row, and Shuffle's pool, through the capsule's garments; the strips' next pages take `?capsule=`. The outfit gallery (#9) plugs in the same way: `/outfits/ideas?capsule=` draws its pool through `inCapsule`, and the capsule page's "Swipe outfits" (the owner's) opens it.
- **The API** (#194b; `api-routes.ts`, resources in `capsule-resource.ts`, `src/web/api/AGENTS.md`): every page write has a twin through the same `capsule-actions.ts` call, with `?ownerId=` as the pages. `GET /capsules` (the cards, sliced by an `offsetCursor`: the one statement reads them all), `GET /capsules/{id}` (name and notes; its garments are `GET /garments?capsuleId=`), `POST` (201; a blank or taken name a 400 naming `body/name`, as the form), `PUT` (both fields replaced), `DELETE`. Membership is the writer's own shape, a set change from either side: `PATCH /capsules/{id}/members` and `PATCH /garments/{id}/capsules` with `{ add, remove }` (MCP's `set_capsule_membership` too), ids outside the wardrobe dropped, never one pair per call (decisions 2026-11-13, #194b).

## Gotchas

- **A capsule only ever holds its owner's garments, and the capsule filter relies on it.** `inCapsule` is applied without checking whose capsule it is (the tiles sentinel, the picker's `member` per tile, the capsule page's reads beside its lookup, Styling's strip pages): on another wardrobe it matches nothing because `changeMembership` drops every garment of another owner. A second writer of `capsule_garment`, or anything that moves a garment between owners, must keep that, or `?capsule=` starts showing another wardrobe's garments. Pages that render a capsule's name still look it up in the addressed wardrobe (404).
