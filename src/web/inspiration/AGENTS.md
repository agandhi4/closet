# Inspiration

`src/web/inspiration/` (#22; `docs/plans/2026-11-13-inspiration-library.md`): saved looks, the Outfits page's **Inspiration** tab (Saved, Ideas, Inspiration through `OutfitTabs`; `/inspiration` is the `outfits` section in `sections.ts`). A look is a photo, a link, or both, with a title and a note, and the pieces the owner reads in it (`inspiration_piece`, 22b); "Recreate this look" matches them to the closet (22b), saving the match as an outfit and "Find this" (22c), the share target and MCP 22d. **The owner's own, like the style profile**: `sessionUserId` alone, no `?ownerId=`; another user's look or photo is `NotFound('inspiration')` from the writers (a 404 like an unknown id), and nothing reaches a grantee, the share page or another user's MCP tools.

- **Tables** (`drizzle/0049_inspiration.sql`): `inspiration` (`owner_id`, `title`, `note`, `source_url` http(s) only, `photo_id` unique and nullable, `ON DELETE CASCADE` from `file`; `inspiration_photo_or_link_check`: at least one of the two), `inspiration_piece` (`(inspiration_id, position)` key, `role` from `INSPIRATION_PIECE_ROLES`, `type`, `colors[]` (at most `INSPIRATION_PIECE_MAX_COLORS`), `pattern`, all checked against `src/wardrobe/properties.ts`; `matched_garment_id` `ON DELETE SET NULL`: the garment chosen for the piece, a closet garment its outfit was saved with or the wishlist item "Find this" made, written only by the match view's two writes (22c); a piece's edit clears it). Positions are never renumbered: a removal leaves a gap, an add takes the last plus one under the look's row lock; at most `INSPIRATION_MAX_PIECES` (12, a 400 past it).
- **One writer** (`queries.ts`): `saveInspiration` (a new look; with a photo, its `file` row inserted `unwanted` in the same transaction: a look is a whole picture, so no cutout is ever queued), `saveInspirationWithPendingPhoto` (a pasted link's pending photo claimed with the look), `setInspirationPhoto` (locks the look, swaps the photo, deletes the replaced `file` row), `updateInspirationText`, `deleteInspiration` (the look then its `file` row). `writes.ts` holds the commit contract (Images): bytes stored before the transaction, deleted when it fails, unlinked after a commit that replaced or removed them.
- **Three ways in** (two in 22a): the add page's camera or library button (`POST /inspiration`, multipart, `photo-upload.tsx`) and a pasted link (`POST /inspiration/link`). The link goes through `importLink` (Link import: the outbound fetcher, its caps, `blocked-by-site`), under a `LINK_IMPORT_LIMIT` checker of its own; the photo it keeps as a pending photo is claimed in the look's transaction (`saveInspirationWithPendingPhoto`: `lockPhotoName`, `takePendingPhoto`, like a garment's save), and a page's `<title>`/product name becomes the look's title. A site that refuses us (a 502 of `REFUSALS`) or a page with no readable photo still saves the link, landing on its page with `?unread=<reason>` (a one-shot note: add a screenshot). A link the server never fetches (a 400 of `REFUSALS`) is the add page again with the reason, nothing saved. The share target (POST, images) is 22d.
- **Photos are private, like selfies**: served only to their owner by `GET /inspiration/photo/:fileName` and `/inspiration/photo/thumb/:fileName` (`sendPrivatePhoto`, `src/web/files/private-photo.ts`, shared with selfies: a bare 404 for anyone else, `private, immutable`), drawn through `inspirationPhotoUrl` (`PrivatePhoto`), never `imageUrl` (`photo-ref-guard.spec.ts` holds this directory to it). `publicPhoto` refuses their names on `/file/**`; `photoIsReferenced` counts `inspiration.photo_id`, so reconciliation never takes one for an orphan; account deletion takes them with the user's `file` rows. The service worker stores none (no image route matches `/inspiration/photo/`).
- **Pieces** (`piece-form.tsx`, `piece-input.ts`): one select names role and type together (`kind` = `role` or `role/type`, `PIECE_KINDS`, so a type is always its role's and nothing redraws on a role change), then a main colour, a second one and a pattern, each a select ('' none; `property.color.*` labels). A stored piece is an `AutosaveForm` posting to `POST /inspiration/:id/pieces/:position` (status line) beside its own Remove (`.../delete`); a new one is a native post to `POST /inspiration/:id/pieces`, both landing on `#look-pieces`. Anything off the form's options is a 400 (`PieceBody`). **Hints** (`piece-hints.ts`): the title (a pasted page's own until renamed) split where it lists garments and read by link import's `guessKind`/`guessColors`, one "+ Blazer · Blue" post each, never saved without the tap, none for a role and type the look already has.
- **Recreate this look** (`GET /inspiration/:id/recreate`, `recreate-page.tsx`): `recreateInputs` reads the look, its pieces, the owner's closet (`recreateInputsSql` in `src/web/gallery/queries.ts`: inCloset with `available` and the rest, the avoided pairs, the outfit rules) in **one statement after the session's**; `recreateLook` (`src/wardrobe/recreate.ts`, The garment model) ranks and re-picks. Each piece is a listbox `SnapStrip` (the chosen match centred, up to two alternates, then None), a missing piece says so, a re-picked one says the closest clashed, and `unfixed` pieces get one warning. The owner's closet only, never a grantee's. The look's pieces carry their `matched` garment in the same statement: `recreateLook` pins it (`saved`): while it still matches the piece it is that piece's chosen garment, always among its matches and offered to no other piece, so the defaults are unique; the save refuses (409) one garment posted for two pieces; and a piece whose match is on the wishlist takes no part in the `recreateLook` assignment (`recreateClosetPieces` in `routes.tsx`: it would reserve a garment a later piece needs) and shows "On your wishlist" with its item, no strip.
- **Save as outfit and Find this** (22c; `recreate-writes.ts`, `addPieceToWishlist` in `queries.ts`, `piece-wishlist.ts`): the match view is **one native `PostForm`** to `POST /inspiration/:id/recreate/outfit`; each strip posts a `position` hidden input, then its own `garment` (the strip's input, '' for None), pairs in document order (unequal counts, a piece twice: 400). "Find this" on a missing piece is a submit button of the same form with `formaction` (`POST /inspiration/:id/pieces/:position/find`; forms cannot nest), so the form has no text field: Enter never picks it as the default button. **`saveRecreation`** is one owner transaction: `lockLookForSave` (the look `FOR UPDATE`, then **its own statement** for the pieces' roles: one joined statement would read them as they were before a waited-for edit; every piece write, `updateInspirationPiece` included, takes the same `lockOwnLook` lock), `pickIdea` (closet garments only, top to toe, **once per garment set**, so a double tap answers `?alreadySaved=1`; named after the look's title, else as an idea), then `recordPieceChoices` (one `UPDATE ... FROM (VALUES ...)` storing a garment only if it is the owner's and of the piece's `pieceCategory`). A choice for a piece the look no longer has, or of another kind, is a 409 (`LOOK_CHANGED`) rolling back the outfit too; a garment gone from the closet is pickIdea's `garmentsGoneError`; all None is a 400. 10 statements with the session. **`addPieceToWishlist`** (in `queries.ts`: only queries open a transaction, `make guard-check`'s own-tx): the piece row `FOR UPDATE`, then (its own statement, so it sees what a waiting twin committed) whether its match is already an own wishlist item, answered without a write; else `insertGarment` with status `wishlist` (`wishlistFieldsOf`: the role's category, the type as its name and its presets, the colours, the pattern where the category takes one, a note naming the look and its link) and the piece pointed at it. 6 statements. **Not the look's link as the item's product link** (the plan said so): a lookbook page is not where the piece is sold, and two pieces of one look would be one product to Muse's never-again rule. "Save with wishlist pieces" (plan §4) is not built: the outfit holds closet garments only.
- **Pages**: the list (`list-page.tsx`, one statement after the session's, newest first; a tile is the thumb in a 4:5 frame or, for a link alone, its site), the add page (`new-page.tsx`, `FormPage`: the photo buttons above, the link field its one post), the look's page (`show-page.tsx`: the photo or the buttons to add one, Change photo, the link in a new tab, title and note in an `AutosaveForm`, Delete with a confirm). Every write is a native `PostForm`, disabled offline.
- **Logs** (logger `Web`): `Inspiration <id> saved as outfit <id> by user <id> with <n> garment(s)`, `... recreated by user <id>: its <n> garment(s) are already outfit <id>, nothing created`, `Inspiration <id> piece <n> added to the wishlist of user <id> as garment <id>`, `... already on the wishlist ... nothing created`, `Inspiration <id> piece <n> (<kind>) added|saved by user <id>`, `Inspiration <id> piece <n> removed by user <id>`, `Inspiration <id> saved by user <id> from an upload: <file>`, `... from a link (<page|image>): <file>`, `... from a link without its photo (<reason>)`, `Inspiration <id> photo added|replaced ...`, `Inspiration <id> deleted by user <id> with its photo <file>`.
- **Specs**: `test/integration/inspiration.spec.ts` (add, list, the link's outcomes, autosave, photo swap, delete, reconciliation, tagging and its 400s, the cap, the match view's one statement, the save and its refusals, Find this and its double tap), `src/wardrobe/recreate.spec.ts` (the matching), `piece-hints.spec.ts`, the matrix rows in `authorization-outfits.routes.ts`, the pages in `pages.ts`.
