# Calendar

`src/web/calendar/`. A calendar day is a plain date: `outfit_calendar.day` is a Postgres `date`, a `'YYYY-MM-DD'` string everywhere in TypeScript, and all arithmetic is on day numbers in `src/calendar-date.ts` (pure, shared by the pure layers, so it lives beside `random.ts`). No JS `Date` represents a calendar day (DST mislabels days). The one conversion from an instant is `todayIn(APP_TIMEZONE, now)`: "today" is the household's date in `APP_TIMEZONE` (IANA name, default `America/New_York`, checked at boot), never UTC or the container's zone. Weeks run Sunday to Saturday. `calendar-view.ts` builds the page model from plain dates (pure, unit-tested in New York time).

- **Routes**: `GET /calendar[?week=YYYY-MM-DD]` (missing or malformed is the current week: navigation state, never a 400; `?calMonth=` is ignored); `GET /calendar/month[?month=YYYY-MM]` (owner only; malformed is this month; network first, not a tab root); `GET /calendar/plan?for=day:D&occasion=O[&replace=E]` (missing or malformed destination is today, all day; a `replace` not the user's entry there plans one more); `POST /calendar` (`date`, `outfitId`, optional `occasion` (all day without one), `week` and `replace` (an entry id: change its outfit); 302 to the week; malformed input, an unknown occasion included, is a 400); `POST /calendar/:id/delete` (`navigateTo` the week to htmx, else 303) and `/worn` (every `WornControl` on an entry: the entry's whole `OccasionRow` to htmx, read by `findEntry` as the week reads it so marks, label and Change follow the worn state; else 303), whose optional body only picks the redirect: `returnTo` through `safeReturnTo` (the calendar's day, `/`, the trip), else the posted week (pills cached before #359). Entries are the signed-in user's own: 404 for no such entry and for someone else's. **The surfaces reach an entry only through `entry-actions.ts`** (#188d: `planOutfit`, `markEntryWorn`, `requireEntry` (the worn row's redraw), `removeEntry`) and `replaceEntryOutfit` (the pages, MCP and `/api/v1/calendar-entries`, `api-routes.ts`, #193b; the API names an entry by id alone, so `EntryTarget.day` is optional and the replace answers the entry's day; its `PUT .../outfit` takes an `outfitId` or an idea's `garmentIds`, `entryOutfitChoice`, #193c; `GET` with `to` alone is the week up to it, #570), each taking the `Actor` and throwing `NotFound('calendarEntry')` / `NotFound('outfit')`, never a 403: a share never reaches an entry (`entry-actions.spec.ts`).
- **Scheduling is idempotent**: `UNIQUE (owner_id, day, outfit_id)`, and the one writer, `insertEntry` (`src/web/calendar/queries.ts`, taking a `Queryable`), inserts with `ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE ... WHERE`: **a person's write (`planned_by` `user`, the default) that meets the week planner's `auto` entry of that outfit on that day takes it over** (`planned_by` becomes `user`, nothing else; the answer is `already-scheduled` with `adopted`, told from an insert by `xmax = 0` in `RETURNING`), and the planner's `auto` write never downgrades a person's entry. Callers: `scheduleOutfit` (POST /calendar and schedule_outfit through `planOutfit`, ownership check first), the outfit form's save transaction, the gallery's pick (and "Plan my week", `planned_by` 'auto'), the seed. The unique index leads with `(owner_id, day)`, so it also serves the week query and the owner foreign key.
- **Owner lock**: **every write to `outfit_calendar`, the week planner's tables (`week_plan`, `week_plan_entry`, `week_replan`) and Muse's needs and suggestions (`option_group`, a suggestion's columns) runs in `ownerTransaction`** (`src/web/auth/queries.ts`: a transaction (a savepoint in a caller's) whose first statement is `lockOwner`, the owner's `user` row `FOR NO KEY UPDATE` plus the lock timeout; **nested in an `ownerTransaction` of the same owner it joins it**: no savepoint, no second lock, so a nested writer's failure is its caller's). The writers take it themselves: `insertEntry`, `scheduleOutfit`, `wearOutfitOn`, `setEntryWorn`, `setEntrySelfie`, `deleteEntry`, `replaceEntryOutfit`, `createOutfit` (always), `updateOutfit`, `deleteOutfit`, `pickIdea`, `planMyWeek`, `undoWeekPlan`, the re-plan (and its claim after a failure), `decide`, `markSuggestion`, `createOptionGroup`, `buyWishlistItem`, and the repair log's `addRepair` and `deleteRepair` (`src/web/wardrobe/repairs.ts`, for the bounded waits); the helpers below them (`setEntryOutfit`, `removeAutoEntries`, `recordAutoEntry`, ...) are called only inside one. **Why a lock, not conflict-safe statements**: the re-plan, Undo and a replace read several rows, judge, then write; `ON CONFLICT` makes one statement safe but cannot keep a judgement true. **Order**: the owner lock before any row lock of the transaction (an entry, an outfit, a garment, a trip), because the re-plan and Undo lock outfits under it: `updateOutfit` and `deleteOutfit` take it before the outfit, `buyWishlistItem` before the garment. **Every write to a synced table takes it first (#196a)**, in `ownerTransaction` or folded into its first statement; how, and the test-only guard that holds it: `src/db/AGENTS.md`, The owner lock's statement. A new writer uses `ownerTransaction(db, ownerId, '<its name>', work)`; `test/integration/owner-lock.spec.ts` holds one writer open while the other waits. **Exempt, because they judge nothing**: `pruneReplanClaims` (nightly; no writer reads old `week_replan` rows). **Bounded waits**: every form of the owner lock (`lockOwner` and the folded ones) sets `lock_timeout` to `OWNER_LOCK_TIMEOUT_MS` (5 s, `SET LOCAL`, so nested it lasts to the caller's commit); past it the writer throws `OwnerLockTimeout` (`src/web/errors.tsx`), a 503 "still saving, try again" page, logged `-> 503: ... (<writer> for owner <id> waited 5000 ms for a lock)` from `ownerTransaction`, `(a write waited 5000 ms for a lock)` from a folded lock (`describeError` maps its 55P03); the re-plan defers to the next minute instead. The pool's `statement_timeout` and `idle_in_transaction_session_timeout` (`src/db/AGENTS.md`) end a stalled holder. A missing owner row is a 404, not an unlocked write. **A savepoint that took the lock first and rolled back releases it**: a caller that catches a nested `ownerTransaction`'s failure and carries on is unlocked from there (none does; take `lockOwner` first if one must).
- **Occasions**: `outfit_calendar.occasion` (text, default and check constraint from `src/wardrobe/occasions.ts`) is the part of the day an entry is for: `all-day`, `workout`, `work`, `daytime`, `evening`, `night-out`, in that display order. `OCCASION_HINTS` gives each a weather window (hours in `APP_TIMEZONE`, e.g. work 8 to 18) and a formality range on the garment scale (work 3 to 4, workout 1): the one source for the weather and the generator. **Several outfits a day are separate entries; the same outfit is on a day once**, whatever the occasion: planning it again for another occasion inserts nothing and keeps the first occasion (so a form re-saved at the default never moves an evening entry to all day). No "change an entry's occasion" write yet: delete and plan again. Labels: `occasionLabel` (`src/web/date-labels.ts`, catalog `occasion.<value>`).
- **Changing an entry's outfit**: `replaceEntryOutfit` (`replace.ts`), the one replace write, reached from POST /calendar's `replace`, the gallery's pick with `replace` and `schedule_outfit`'s `replaceEntryId`. `replaceEntryOutfit(db, actor, target, choice)` answers what it changed or throws its refusal with nothing written (#188d; no outcome union for callers to translate). One owner transaction: the entry (the owner's, on that day and, when given, that occasion; else 404) locked, then the outfit (`ownsOutfit`, or an idea via `pickIdea`), then `setEntryOutfit` (the entry keeps its id, day and occasion). Rules: **a worn entry is refused** (409: it records that day's wears; Change shows on unworn entries only); **its selfie is kept as a look on its day** (`detachEntrySelfie`); **the entry becomes the person's** (`planned_by` 'user'), also when the choice is the outfit it already has (an `auto` entry taken over, `adopted`, logged `... already holds outfit <id>; taken over from the week planner by user <id>`); **an outfit "Plan my week" created for it** goes once nothing holds it (`removeUnheldOutfits`, `outfitIsHeld`), only while the entry was `auto`; **the new outfit already on that day** is refused (409, naming its occasion). Idempotent: a double tap's second request waits on the lock and finds `unchanged` ("Already saved"). Logs (logger `Web`): `Calendar entry <id> (<day> <occasion>) changed by user <id>: outfit <a> -> [new ]outfit <b>[, selfie <id> kept as a look][, planner's outfit <a> removed]`, `... already holds outfit <id> ...; nothing changed`; a refusal is the error line with its `logDetail`, `... -> 409: ... (Calendar entry <id> (<day> <occasion>) not changed for user <id>: <reason>)`. `test/integration/replace-entry.spec.ts`.
