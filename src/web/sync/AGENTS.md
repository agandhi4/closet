# The changes feed

`GET /api/v1/changes?cursor=&limit=` (#196b; `listChanges`): a native client's sync, every synced row and deletion after its cursor, across the caller's own wardrobe and each one shared with them. No page twin: the PWA reads pages. The rows are stamped by the sync triggers (`src/db/AGENTS.md`, The sync triggers); the plan is PR #605.

- **Files**: `feed.ts` (`readChanges`, the registry `FEED`), `cursor.ts` (the position, its encoding, `isExpired`), `queries.ts` (the wardrobes read, the keyset read), `api-routes.ts`.
- **One `REPEATABLE READ READ ONLY` transaction per page**: the wardrobes (self, then each accepted share by owner id, `acceptedSharesWith`) with `revision` and `sync_floor`, then one keyset `union all` (a branch per resource and one for `sync_tombstone`, per wardrobe, each on its `(owner, revision)` index), then one batched read per resource on the page. 6 statements for a page of garments at any `limit`, 8 for all three resources, 5 for an empty page (`api-statements.spec.ts`). Never a per-row read.
- **Order within a wardrobe: `(revision, kind, resource, id, seq)`**: kind 0 a deletion, 1 a row (a revision's deletions first); `resource` the table, compared `COLLATE "C"` on both sides; `seq` the tombstone's id (0 for a row). A page fills one wardrobe before the next. Positions only move forward; a row rewritten between pages gets a higher revision and comes again (a resend, never a skip).
- **The end is exclusive** (`endOf`, kind 2): a finished wardrobe resumes after the snapshot's `user.revision`, so a wardrobe still at the backfill's 0 is not resent forever. The end comes from the snapshot, never the highest row seen: a revision commits with its rows under the owner lock, so nothing at or below it is in flight.
- **Expiry** (`isExpired`): a position before the end of `sync_floor`'s deletions is a 410 `cursor_expired` (full sync), unless its pass began at or above the floor (`Position.floor`: a full sync paging through rows below the floor must not restart forever). A fresh read leaves out tombstones at or below the floor. So does a cursor answered when the registry carried fewer resources (`FEED_COVERAGE`): it never saw their older rows.
- **A full sync is self-describing** (`snapshot`): every page of a sync begun without a cursor carries `snapshot: { wardrobes }` (the caller's own, then each accepted share), held across pages by the cursor's `s: 1` until the page where `more` is false. After that page the client drops every synced row not re-sent and every wardrobe not listed, since the fresh read sends no deletions at or below the floor and no `revokedWardrobes`. A 410 mid-way restarts it from no cursor.
- **A grantee's wardrobe** carries only `shared` entries (garments, every status, and capsules), its garments in the grantee shape (no `care`, no `repairs`: `garmentsByIds` reads the log only where the viewer owns it). A cursor naming an owner the caller no longer sees drops it and lists it in `revokedWardrobes`. `?ownerId=` is a 400 (the closed query).
- **The garment carries its stored fields only** (`SyncedGarment`): no `worn`, `wornSinceWash`, `lastWorn`, `cost`, `costPerWear` or `capsules`, whose sources never move its revision; the client derives them from the wears and capsules (decisions 2026-11-13).
- **Adding a resource** (#608): one `FEED` entry keyed by its `sync-tables.ts` table, taking it off `PENDING_FEED_RESOURCES` (tsc and `feed.spec.ts` hold the two to `SYNC_ROOTS`): its API `name`, `shared`, its titled feed schema beside its API resource, and a batched `…ByIds(db, ids)` reader beside its per-id one, one statement for any owners. Regenerate `openapi.json`, extend `api-changes.spec.ts` and the statements pin.
- **Tests**: `test/integration/api-changes.spec.ts` (the delta across pages, a page boundary inside one revision, the backfill, a held writer, the floor, a full sync's snapshot, a grantee's feed); `feed.spec.ts` (the registry, `isExpired`).
