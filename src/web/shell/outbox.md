# The outbox: queued writes offline (#178)

Plan: `docs/plans/2026-11-12-offline-queued-writes.md` (owner-approved 2026-11-13). The recorded exception to frontend-pwa.md's "unreachable writes are disabled, never dropped": Wore today (and its undo), Washed, the laundry's batch, an entry's worn mark (and Undo) and a trip's packing and extras ticks are kept while offline and replayed when the server answers again. Everything else stays `data-needs-network`.

## The pieces

- **Pure rules** (`src/offline-writes.ts`, unit-tested): the kinds and their routes (`QUEUEABLE_ROUTES`), the collapse key and path of a record (`queuedTarget`: `wear:41:<day>`, `wash:41`, `entry:88`, `packed:<trip>:<garment>`, `item:<trip>:<item>`), the replay allowlist (`queueableKindOf`), the answer table (`replayVerdict`), the backoff (`retryDelayMs`) and the cap (`OUTBOX_MAX_PENDING`, 200). The server's views and the page's outbox both use them.
- **Markup** (`src/web/outbox.tsx`): a queueable form carries `data-offline-queue="<kind>"` instead of `data-needs-network` (`queueForm`, or `PostForm`/`AutosaveForm`'s `offlineQueue`), so `connectivity.js` leaves it enabled. A region (`outboxRegion`: `data-outbox-key`, `-path`, `-label`, and Wore today's `-day`) is one record: around a form, that form's write; inside a form (the laundry's rows, the packing lists' rows), each row by itself. `OutboxAlternate` is the other state in a `<template data-outbox-state>`; `OutboxPending` is "Waiting to sync", shown by `main.css` under `data-outbox="pending"`. Integration: `test/integration/queued-writes.spec.ts`; read a state's absence through `shownMarkup` (harness), since the template holds the other one.
- **The page** (`views/assets/outbox.ts`, bundled by `npm run generate:outbox` into `public/js/outbox.js`, gitignored; `build`, `start:dev` and the typecheck run it): loaded on signed-in pages, after the importmap (it imports `toast` and `single-flight` from it, kept external by esbuild). IndexedDB `closet-outbox`, store `writes`, keyed by `id`, indexed by `key` and `account`. Strings: `data-text-outbox-*` on `#app-status`, with `data-account` and `data-time-zone`; the banner (`#outbox-banner`) and the notice (`#outbox-notice`) are `AppStatus`'s.

## Behavior

- **Queueing**: while `<html data-connectivity>` is offline or reconnecting, or online while any of the tap's keys has a pending record (queued, backing off or in flight: `goesThroughOutbox`, the one rule, in `mustQueue`), an htmx queueable request is cancelled at `htmx:confirm` and a native one at a capture-phase `submit` (before `submit-once.js`); an htmx one that ends in `htmx:sendError` is queued too (it may have landed: the replay is idempotent). A native post in the window before the heartbeat notices still lands on the offline page. One transaction deletes the account's record with the same key and puts the new one: the last intent wins (`collapsed` is the same rule over the tab's in-memory records, updated at the tap so the next tap's routing sees it). Online, the queued tap is drained at once, after a drain in flight (single-flight). A tap sent straight past a pending record would land and then be overwritten by the older record's replay (Wore today, a 503, Undo in the backoff: worn again). Wore today and Washed post the tap's `day` (`todayIn` APP_TIMEZONE); a page rendered another day refuses to queue Wore today ("reload it"). A worn `wear` posts its record id as `clientId`.
- **Showing**: the tapped region swaps in its template's state; a checklist row's checkbox is its own state. After every load, `htmx:afterSettle` and store change, each region with a pending record gets `data-outbox="pending"`, and one rendered before the record (a cached page reopened) shows the record's state. Counts and lines stay the server's until the sync. Banner: "N changes waiting to sync" offline, "Syncing N changes" only past 2 s, "Sign in to save N changes" after a 401, the cap's reason past 200 (the queueable controls are then disabled, marked `data-outbox-full`).
- **Replay**: on `connectivity:change` to online, on page load and on `visibilitychange` to visible while online. One drainer: `single-flight` in the tab, `navigator.locks` across tabs (a tab finding it held waits, then drains what is left, so a tap there is not stranded); both lock and `crypto.randomUUID` need a secure context (the HTTP twin is not), so ids come from `getRandomValues` and without locks a tab drains alone. Oldest first, one at a time, as a same-origin `fetch` with `X-Closet-Queued: <account>` (`REPLAY_HEADER`) and `Idempotency-Key: <id>` (CSRF applies unchanged); a record replaced since the drain read the store is skipped, and an answer is written back only to a record still queued (`settle`), so an older intent never returns beside its successor; only a path `queueableKindOf` accepts is posted, anything else is deleted and logged. 204 deletes the record; 404, 409 and every other unexpected answer mark it `failed` with its reason; a network error, 5xx, 408 or 429 stops the drain, keeps the order and retries (5 s doubling to 5 min); a 401, or an opaque redirect (status 0), stops it and keeps everything. The session gate answers a replay the 401 by its header, not Fetch Metadata, which the plain-HTTP twin never sends (`session-access.ts`). After a sync: a toast "N changes saved" and `outbox:synced`, on which `freshness.js` asks `REVALIDATE_PAGE` (swapped in only if untouched).
- **Failures** stay in the notice ("Navy chinos, Wore today: not saved, it was deleted.") until Dismiss deletes them.
- **Accounts**: a page of another account deletes every other account's record unsent (logged as a count); a signed-out page loads no outbox, so signing back in as the same account replays them. Signing out drains, then names what is left (failed or unreachable) in a confirm and deletes it before posting the logout; declining stays signed in.

## Not here yet (178c)

The worker deletes the store at its session boundaries (`claimPageCache` for another account, logout, account deletion) and refetches the synced paths' cached copies (`OUTBOX_SYNCED`); until then the page's own rules above are the boundary, and a warmed page reopens with the server's old state until it is revalidated.
