# The outbox: queued writes offline (#178)

Plan: `docs/plans/2026-11-12-offline-queued-writes.md` (owner-approved 2026-11-13). The recorded exception to frontend-pwa.md's "unreachable writes are disabled, never dropped": Wore today (and its undo), Washed, the laundry's batch, an entry's worn mark (and Undo) and a trip's packing and extras ticks are kept while offline and replayed when the server answers again. Everything else stays `data-needs-network`.

## The pieces

- **Pure rules** (`src/offline-writes.ts`, unit-tested): the kinds and their routes (`QUEUEABLE_ROUTES`), the collapse key and path of a record (`queuedTarget`: `wear:41:<day>`, `wash:41`, `entry:88`, `packed:<trip>:<garment>`, `item:<trip>:<item>`), the replay allowlist (`queueableKindOf`), the answer table (`replayVerdict`), the backoff (`retryDelayMs`), the replay timeout (`REPLAY_TIMEOUT_MS`, 15 s), the cap (`OUTBOX_MAX_PENDING`, 200), and the queue over its store (`OutboxStore`, `routesThroughOutbox`, `enqueue`: unit-tested with a stub, a failing put included). The server's views and the page's outbox both use them.
- **Markup** (`src/web/outbox.tsx`): a queueable form carries `data-offline-queue="<kind>"` instead of `data-needs-network` (`queueForm`, or `PostForm`/`AutosaveForm`'s `offlineQueue`), so `connectivity.js` leaves it enabled. A region (`outboxRegion`: `data-outbox-key`, `-path`, `-label`, and Wore today's `-day`) is one record: around a form, that form's write; inside a form (the laundry's rows, the packing lists' rows), each row by itself. `OutboxAlternate` is the other state in a `<template data-outbox-state>`; `OutboxPending` is "Waiting to sync", shown by `main.css` under `data-outbox="pending"`. Integration: `test/integration/queued-writes.spec.ts`; read a state's absence through `shownMarkup` (harness), since the template holds the other one.
- **The page** (`views/assets/outbox.ts`, bundled by `npm run generate:outbox` into `public/js/outbox.js`, gitignored; `build`, `start:dev` and the typecheck run it): loaded on signed-in pages, after the importmap (it imports `toast` and `single-flight` from it, kept external by esbuild). IndexedDB `closet-outbox`, store `writes`, keyed by `id`, indexed by `key` and `account`. Strings: `data-text-outbox-*` on `#app-status`, with `data-account` and `data-time-zone`; the banner (`#outbox-banner`) and the notice (`#outbox-notice`) are `AppStatus`'s.

## Behavior

- **The model**: IndexedDB is the only truth. No tab keeps a copy of the records: every tap decision, the cap check, sign-out's count and every render read the store as committed, so a put that failed leaves nothing pending and another tab's record counts at once. Every write (`write()`) posts on the `closet-outbox` BroadcastChannel; the other tabs re-read and re-render, and after a put drain if they can reach the server. A record is pending only once its put has committed. One scheduler (`schedule()`) starts every drain, whatever woke it; offline it does nothing until connectivity.js's change to online.
- **Queueing**: every submit of a queueable form is held (htmx: `htmx:confirm` cancelled, a native one at a capture-phase `submit`, before `submit-once.js`) while the store answers `routesThroughOutbox`: offline or reconnecting per `<html data-connectivity>`, or online while any of the tap's keys has a pending record in any tab (queued, backing off or in flight), it is queued; otherwise sent (`issueRequest(true)`, or `requestSubmit` let through once). Taps are decided one at a time per tab (`serially`), so a second tap reads the first one's committed put; a native double tap meanwhile is dropped. An htmx one that ends in `htmx:sendError` is queued too (it may have landed: the replay is idempotent) and drained by the scheduler, since the probe after it may still say online and fire no change. A native post in the window before the heartbeat notices still lands on the offline page. One transaction deletes the account's record with the same key and puts the new one: the last intent wins. A tap sent straight past a pending record would land and then be overwritten by the older record's replay (Wore today, a 503, Undo in the backoff: worn again). Wore today and Washed post the tap's `day` (`todayIn` APP_TIMEZONE); a page rendered another day refuses to queue Wore today ("reload it"). A worn `wear` posts its record id as `clientId`.
- **Showing**: re-derived from the store after every load, `htmx:afterSettle`, connectivity change and store change (this tab's or another's). A region with a pending record gets `data-outbox="pending"` and shows the record's state: a checklist row its tick, a control its template's state whenever the shown form would post what is queued (so the tap, a cached page reopened, and Wore today then Undo all come out right). `data-outbox-intent` (`flipped` or `ticked`) says the region shows an intent, not the server's markup: when its record ends failed the region is put back (flipped again, or the checkbox to its `defaultChecked`); when it is saved the mark just goes. Counts and lines stay the server's until the sync. Banner: "N changes waiting to sync" offline, "Syncing N changes" only past 2 s, "Sign in to save N changes" after a 401, the cap's reason past 200 (the queueable controls are then disabled, marked `data-outbox-full`).
- **Replay** (`schedule()`): after every enqueue, on `connectivity:change` to online, on page load, on `visibilitychange` to visible, on another tab's put and on its own backoff timer (set only by `drain`, one per tab). One drainer: `single-flight` in the tab, `navigator.locks` across tabs (a tab finding it held waits, then drains what is left, so a tap there is not stranded; each replay is aborted after `REPLAY_TIMEOUT_MS` by a `setTimeout`, not `AbortSignal.timeout`, so Playwright's clock can jump it, and so the lock always comes back); both lock and `crypto.randomUUID` need a secure context (the HTTP twin is not), so ids come from `getRandomValues` and without locks a tab drains alone. Oldest first, one at a time, as a same-origin `fetch` with `X-Closet-Queued: <account>` (`REPLAY_HEADER`) and `Idempotency-Key: <id>` (CSRF applies unchanged); a record replaced since the drain read the store is skipped, and an answer is written back only to a record still queued (`settle`), so an older intent never returns beside its successor; only a path `queueableKindOf` accepts is posted, anything else is deleted and logged. 204 deletes the record; 404, 409 and every other unexpected answer mark it `failed` with its reason; a network error, timeout, 5xx, 408 or 429 stops the drain, keeps the order and retries (5 s doubling to 5 min); a 401, or an opaque redirect (status 0), stops it and keeps everything. The session gate answers a replay the 401 by its header, not Fetch Metadata, which the plain-HTTP twin never sends (`session-access.ts`). After a sync: a toast "N changes saved" when any was, and `outbox:synced` when any was saved or failed, on which `freshness.js` asks `REVALIDATE_PAGE` (swapped in only if untouched).
- **Failures** stay in the notice ("Navy chinos, Wore today: not saved, it was deleted.") until Dismiss deletes them.
- **Accounts**: a page of another account deletes every other account's record unsent (logged as a count); a signed-out page loads no outbox, so signing back in as the same account replays them. Signing out (held at its `submit` on every signed-in page while the store is read) drains for at most 5 s (`SIGN_OUT_DRAIN_MS`), then names what is left (failed, unreachable or still in flight) in a confirm and deletes it before posting the logout; declining stays signed in.

## Not here yet (178c)

The worker deletes the store at its session boundaries (`claimPageCache` for another account, logout, account deletion) and refetches the synced paths' cached copies (`OUTBOX_SYNCED`); until then the page's own rules above are the boundary, and a warmed page reopens with the server's old state until it is revalidated.
