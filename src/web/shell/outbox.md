# The outbox: queued writes offline (#178)

Plan: `docs/plans/2026-11-12-offline-queued-writes.md` (owner-approved 2026-11-13). The recorded exception to frontend-pwa.md's "unreachable writes are disabled, never dropped": Wore today (and its undo), Washed, the laundry's batch, an entry's worn mark (and Undo) and a trip's packing and extras ticks are kept while offline and replayed when the server answers again. Everything else stays `data-needs-network`.

## The pieces

- **Pure rules** (`src/offline-writes.ts`, unit-tested): the kinds and their routes (`QUEUEABLE_ROUTES`), the collapse key and path of a record (`queuedTarget`: `wear:41:<day>`, `wash:41`, `entry:88`, `packed:<trip>:<garment>`, `item:<trip>:<item>`), the replay allowlist (`queueableKindOf`), the answer table (`replayVerdict`), the drain's rules (`replayQueue`, driven by the page; stuck after `STUCK_AFTER_SERVER_ERRORS` 3 consecutive 5xx, retried every `STUCK_RETRY_MS` 30 min, `isStuck`, `unstuck`), the backoff (`retryDelayMs`), the replay timeout (`REPLAY_TIMEOUT_MS`, 15 s), the cap (`OUTBOX_MAX_PENDING`, 200), and the queue over its store (`OutboxStore`, `routesThroughOutbox`, `enqueue`: unit-tested with a stub, a failing put included). The server's views and the page's outbox both use them.
- **Markup** (`src/web/outbox.tsx`): a queueable form carries `data-offline-queue="<kind>"` instead of `data-needs-network` (`queueForm`, or `PostForm`/`AutosaveForm`'s `offlineQueue`), so `connectivity.js` leaves it enabled. A region (`outboxRegion`: `data-outbox-key`, `-path`, `-label`, and Wore today's `-day`) is one record: around a form, that form's write; inside a form (the laundry's rows, the packing lists' rows), each row by itself. `OutboxAlternate` is the other state in a `<template data-outbox-state>`; `OutboxPending` is "Waiting to sync", shown by `main.css` under `data-outbox="pending"`. Integration: `test/integration/queued-writes.spec.ts`; read a state's absence through `shownMarkup` (harness), since the template holds the other one.
- **The store** (`views/assets/outbox-store.ts`): the database's one definition (name, version, schema, the channel), imported by the page's outbox and the worker, since whichever opens it first creates it.
- **The page** (`views/assets/outbox.ts`, bundled by `npm run generate:outbox` into `public/js/outbox.js`, gitignored; `build`, `start:dev` and the typecheck run it): loaded on signed-in pages, after the importmap (it imports `toast` and `single-flight` from it, kept external by esbuild). IndexedDB `closet-outbox`, store `writes`, keyed by `id`, indexed by `key` and `account`. Strings: `data-text-outbox-*` on `#app-status`, with `data-account` and `data-time-zone`; the banner (`#outbox-banner`) and the notice (`#outbox-notice`) are `AppStatus`'s.

## Behavior

- **The model**: IndexedDB is the only truth. No tab keeps a copy of the records: every tap decision, the cap check, sign-out's count and every render read the store as committed, so a put that failed leaves nothing pending and another tab's record counts at once. Every write (`write()`) posts on the `closet-outbox` BroadcastChannel; the other tabs re-read and re-render, and after a put drain if they can reach the server. A record is pending only once its put has committed. One scheduler (`schedule()`) starts every drain, whatever woke it; offline it does nothing until connectivity.js's change to online.
- **Queueing**: every submit of a queueable form is held (htmx: `htmx:confirm` cancelled, a native one at a capture-phase `submit`, before `submit-once.js`) while the store answers `routesThroughOutbox`: offline or reconnecting per `<html data-connectivity>`, or online while any of the tap's keys has a pending record in any tab (queued, backing off or in flight), it is queued; otherwise sent (`issueRequest(true)`, or `requestSubmit` let through once). Taps are decided one at a time per tab (`serially`), so a second tap reads the first one's committed put; a native double tap meanwhile is dropped. An htmx one that ends in `htmx:sendError` or `htmx:timeout` (`HTMX_CONFIG`'s, `REPLAY_TIMEOUT_MS`; `request-failures.js` toasts no queueable form's) is queued too (it may have landed: the replay is idempotent) and drained by the scheduler, since the probe after it may still say online and fire no change. A native post in the window before the heartbeat notices still lands on the offline page. One transaction deletes the account's record with the same key and puts the new one: the last intent wins. A tap sent straight past a pending record would land and then be overwritten by the older record's replay (Wore today, a 503, Undo in the backoff: worn again). Wore today and Washed post the tap's `day` (`todayIn` APP_TIMEZONE), and they and the entry worn its instant `at` (ISO, #676; `takesDay`, `takesInstant`), so a same-day wear and wash replay in their taps' order; a record queued before #676 has none, and the server stores its replay's instant as unknown (`tapInstant`: the day rule), never the replay's time; a page rendered another day refuses to queue Wore today ("reload it"). A worn `wear` posts its record id as `clientId`, which the wear keeps (#196a-2); a replay answered 410 (that wear landed, then was deleted since) is saved, unchanged (`replayVerdict`).
- **Showing**: re-derived from the store after every load, `htmx:afterSettle`, connectivity change and store change (this tab's or another's). A region with a pending record gets `data-outbox="pending"` and shows the record's state: a checklist row its tick, a control its template's state whenever the shown form would post what is queued (so the tap, a cached page reopened, and Wore today then Undo all come out right). `data-outbox-intent` (`flipped` or `ticked`) says the region shows an intent, not the server's markup: when its record ends failed the region is put back (flipped again, or the checkbox to its `defaultChecked`); when it is saved the mark just goes. Counts and lines stay the server's until the sync. Banner: "N changes waiting to sync" offline, "Syncing N changes" only past 2 s, "Sign in to save N changes" after a 401, the cap's reason past 200 (the queueable controls are then disabled, marked `data-outbox-full`).
- **Replay** (`schedule()`): after every enqueue, on `connectivity:change` to online, on page load, on `visibilitychange` to visible, on another tab's put and on its own backoff timer (set only by `drain`, one per tab). One drainer: `single-flight` in the tab, `navigator.locks` across tabs (a tab finding it held waits, then drains what is left, so a tap there is not stranded; each replay is aborted after `REPLAY_TIMEOUT_MS` by a `setTimeout`, not `AbortSignal.timeout`, so Playwright's clock can jump it, and so the lock always comes back); both lock and `crypto.randomUUID` need a secure context (the HTTP twin is not), so ids come from `getRandomValues` and without locks a tab drains alone. Oldest first, one at a time, as a same-origin `fetch` with `X-Closet-Queued: <account>` (`REPLAY_HEADER`) and `Idempotency-Key: <id>` (CSRF applies unchanged); a record replaced since the drain read the store is skipped, and an answer is written back only to a record still queued (`settle`), so an older intent never returns beside its successor; only a path `queueableKindOf` accepts is posted, anything else is deleted and logged. 204 deletes the record; 404, 409 and every other unexpected answer mark it `failed` with its reason. **A 5xx never fails or drops a record** (#691, #865, #866, `replayQueue`; no inference about who is at fault): it keeps the record queued and counts (`serverErrors`, optional on the record); at `STUCK_AFTER_SERVER_ERRORS` consecutive answers it is **stuck** (`stuckAt`, which doubles as its retry clock), still `pending` so a newer tap on its key routes through the queue and replaces it. The drain goes on with other records after a 5xx, so one handler bug holds nobody, and a server-wide outage marks several stuck and loses none. A stuck record, and any record with its key, is skipped unless it is due: `stuckAt` is `STUCK_RETRY_MS` old, a record was saved earlier in the drain (the stuck ones are then tried once more at the end), or the drain follows the heartbeat's probe succeeding after a non-online spell (`serverAnswering`: every stuck record is tried in its turn). Still 5xx, it stays stuck. The notice names each stuck write ("..., Wore today: not saved yet, the server keeps answering with an error.") with **Try again** (`unstuck`: clears its counts, drains) and **Discard** (deletes it, its region put back); the drain never ends it itself. **Order**: a record that stays queued (5xx, stuck) holds its key for the rest of the drain, so a record is never sent past an older one with its key; records of other keys behind it go on. A same-day wear and wash are different keys: their order rests on the taps' `day`/`at`, which a record queued before #676 lacks. The network, a timeout, 408, 429 and 502, 503 and 504 (the proxy during a restart, the busy page #643: unavailable, not a poisoned record; only a 500 or another 5xx counts) stop the drain, keep the order, back off (5 s doubling to 5 min) and count nothing. A newer tap on a failed record's key is sent: it is a newer intent and only pending records route through the queue. While records back off online the banner says "not saved yet: the server is not answering"; a 401, or an opaque redirect (status 0), stops it and keeps everything. The session gate answers a replay the 401 by its header, not Fetch Metadata, which the plain-HTTP twin never sends (`session-access.ts`). After a sync: a toast "N changes saved" when any was; `OUTBOX_SYNCED` to the worker with the saved records' paths (below), whose `PAGES_RENEWED` brings the server's copy to the pages on screen. A failure needs no refetch: its region is put back here.
- **Failures** stay in the notice ("Navy chinos, Wore today: not saved, it was deleted.") until Dismiss deletes them; a stuck write is listed there too, with its own Try again and Discard, and Dismiss touches only the failed (a record the #860 client failed with reason `server` is among them: its text stays).
- **Accounts**: a page of another account deletes every other account's record unsent (logged as a count); a signed-out page loads no outbox, so signing back in as the same account replays them. Signing out and deleting the account (held at the `submit` on every signed-in page while the store is read, then resubmitted) drain for at most 5 s (`SIGN_OUT_DRAIN_MS`). Sign-out then names what is left (failed, unreachable or still in flight) in a confirm and deletes it before posting; declining posts nothing. Deletion never asks or discards: the drain runs before `/auth/delete-account`, whose own confirm and password step follow, and what is left is deleted unsent by the next other account's page (`claimForAccount`).

## The worker's part (178c)

The worker never sends a record; it deletes them and refreshes what a sync changed (`views/assets/src-sw.ts`).

- **Deletion**: a page claiming the page cache for a new owner (`claimPageCache`, another account's or after a drop) deletes every other account's records, as the page's own rule does; a signed-out page deletes none (`queuedWritesGoneAtClaim`, unit-tested). A record's `account` (`data-account`) is compared with X-Page-Account as stored, so the two stay one spelling (`queued-writes.spec.ts` holds them equal) or a sign-in deletes the user's own. A sign-out or account deletion is held by the page (`LEAVES_ACCOUNT`, `outbox.ts`): it drains for at most `SIGN_OUT_DRAIN_MS`, for sign-out asks before discarding what is left, then resubmits (deletion only drains); the worker no longer sees either post (#542), so a record left behind (a page that loaded no outbox) stays for the same account's next sign-in, and another account's claim deletes it. A deleted account's writes could never sync; they are deleted unsent by the next other account's claim. Each deletion posts on the channel, so open tabs re-read. Old workers lack this; the page's rules are the backstop either way.
- **Refetch** (`OUTBOX_SYNCED`, `refetchSyncedPages`): the message's paths pass the allowlist (`syncedWritePaths`) or are dropped; every _cached_ page or `|hx` fragment whose pathname shows one (`showsQueuedWrite`: the garment's page and `/laundry` for a wear or wash; `/`, `/calendar` weeks, `/laundry` and trip pages for an entry; the trip's page for a tick) is fetched again through `pageWarmer` (the page plugins: owner, stamp, generation via `runOf`), one at a time, marked `X-Closet-Warm`, the sender's own page included. A renewed copy would leave a later `REVALIDATE_PAGE` nothing new to find, so once done the worker posts every tab it controls the copies whose markup changed (`PAGES_RENEWED`: `url`, `fragment`, `html`, `fetchedAt`), nothing after a drop; `freshness.js` takes the one matching its display (URL and page or fragment) as a revalidation's `updated` (swapped in if untouched, else offered). One mechanism for every tab, the sender's too. Each through `refetchCachedPage`, the activation refresh's own (`updates.md`): its answer table (`refetchVerdict`: 404 and 403 delete the copy, a page's 500 is passed over, 429, 502, 503 and 504 stop, a 401 or `X-Session-Ended` ends the session as a warm's does); unreachable or a drop stops it too. An entry's garments' own pages are not refetched (its path names no garment).
- **Specs**: `src/offline-writes.spec.ts` (the allowlist, the page table, the claim's rule); `test/offline-writes.spec.ts`'s worker step (a page left offline reopens synced; a sender that stayed put has its copy renewed; a second tab on an affected page swaps the server's in). Claim-time deletion has no browser proof of its own: the page's rule acts on the same load.
